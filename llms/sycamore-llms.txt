# Sycamore

> Sycamore is the trusted agent operating system for the enterprise. Build, deploy, and orchestrate autonomous AI agents with enterprise-grade security, human oversight, and organizational intelligence that compounds over time.

Sycamore Labs, Inc. is headquartered in Palo Alto, CA. In March 2026, Sycamore announced that it raised a $65M seed round led by Coatue and Lightspeed, with participation from Abstract Ventures, Dell Technologies Capital, 8VC, Fellows Fund, and E14 Fund.
